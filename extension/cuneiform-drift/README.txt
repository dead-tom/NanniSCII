CUNEIFORM DRIFT — Chrome / Edge / Brave / Arc extension

Screen Decay Trinket · Browser Extension Screen Decay
Particle Overlay > Cuneiform Symbol Melt

A digital mnemonic, subtly transforming text into cuneiform particle decay.
It's an ergonomic trinket from the past, gently reminding us to stay aware of
our passive interchange with online interaction and the living digital archive.

Install (2 minutes)
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (e.g. Documents/cuneiform-drift).
2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions).
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder.
5. Pin the wedge icon to your toolbar. Click it for settings.
6. Open any normal website and press "Show me now" in the popup to see a melt straight away.

Where it works
- Any normal website (http/https). It starts by itself in tabs that were already open.
- Not on Chrome's own pages (new tab, settings, extensions, Chrome Web Store) or built-in PDF viewer: Chrome blocks all extensions there.
- It's meant to be gentle: each melt fades in slowly over about 20 seconds, and they happen now and then, not constantly. Move "Presence" towards Lively for more.

Updating from an earlier version
- Replace the old folder with this one, then press the circular reload arrow on the Cuneiform Drift card in chrome://extensions.

What it does
- Now and then a word lets go of a few tiny clay wedges.
- While you scroll, small wedge marks at the screen edges travel with the page (motion cues), then fade.
- Now and then, at random, a ragged patch of text lets go of its pixels. They swirl together and grow cell by cell into a molten cuneiform sign, which cools, drips down the page, sparkles away, and the text heals. No clicking needed.
- Rarely, a faint cuneiform sign grows in an empty margin and crumbles away.

Controls
- Presence slider: Whisper (a melt about every 18 s) → Lively (about every half second, up to twelve at once; the top quarter of the slider is a showtime boost). Default melts about every 10 s.
- "Pause here" turns it off for just the current site.
- Alt+Shift+K turns it on/off everywhere.
- Respects your system's "reduce motion" setting.
