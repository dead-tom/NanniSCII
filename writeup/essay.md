# The concept behind the project (draft)

An anachronism is an object misplaced in time.

Examined outside its proper temporal domain, the Babylonian tablet transcends almost five thousand years of human communication into the paradigm of modern society as if through a wormhole. As Nanni pressed the familiar sentiment of customer dissatisfaction into clay, the grumbling of exasperation still resonate through epochs of civilisation, through the evolution of language itself, speaking directly to the collective human experience.

Articulating the semantics of expression through language is fundamental in developing our human identity. Yet its true value is realised in our ability to construct interpersonal meaning from an accumulation of experience within the context of the world we live in, for all the paradoxical complexities.

Language is a symbolic examination of the experience of existence, an artefact that reveals our thoughts, values and desires. In The Phenomenology of Spirit, Hegel explores the role of language in the development of human cognition, and argues that language is not simply a tool for communication but rather a fundamental aspect of human thought and consciousness.

Language is living
From reed stylus on clay to quill, printing press and computer, our tools have shaped modes of communication, societal infrastructures and our cognition.  These tools have also enabled us to preserve and build vast records of living voices into an immortal archive of human thought and experience, where every complaint, confession, dream and desire map a topology too immense and too intricate for any human mind to navigate. just as in Borges story“ the library of babel” (1941, the fantasy describe a vast library like a universe who’s expanse

Artificial intelligence has the ability to map the entire human corpus into a geometry of meaning.  Rendering language as a high-dimensional vector space in which every word is defined in statistical relation to every other, it charts the terrain of our collective psyche far beyond the semantics.  It’s at this point that we now step into a new paradigm.  Multimodel AI is no longer a sophisticated “tool” but functions as a forensic operative, capable of excavating symbolic artefacts from statistical residue that reveal more about our species and existence than we can even conceptualise.

as humans we are limited in the amount of information we can process, and limited in the ways we can analyse it all. finding patterns and correlations is limited to 3 dimensions of data, XYZ. But machines are now able to analyse the full picture of 5 thousand years of documentation in trillions of parameters in dimensions we can’t even imagine.

Modern large language models have inverted into a asymetry. where they know more about us then we do about ourselfs, and especially them in an inversion of roles. as this point we ask ourselves does the container become the manipulator? Projective identification, a concept in 1946 by melanie klein and wildfire bion describes a process of unknowing projection onto a receiver, who then in turn embodies the feelings including parts that are unrealised. leading to sa power shift in the dynamic between the receiver or “contaner” and the subject....unfinished return after the generator is made

Five thousand years later the immortalised voices of our past have started speaking back.
